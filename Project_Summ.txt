
Establishes a unified enterprise analytics foundation that advances population health, 
clinical quality, health equity, care management, and outreach through standardized, 
self-service insights for leadership and operations.

These initiatives collectively establish a unified enterprise analytics ecosystem supporting 
population health management, clinical quality, health equity, care management, and outreach, 
while enabling leadership and operational teams with standardized, self-service insights for 
performance monitoring and strategic decision-making.

Provides an on-demand, comprehensive member-level view integrating claims, clinical, pharmacy, care management, SDOH, and predictive analytics data to support intervention population identification and feasibility assessment. (Member 360)

Delivers high-level analytical assessment of populations by Line of Business, Group ID, and 
PMG/Non-PMG, enabling segmentation, cost and utilization profiling, and opportunity identification. 
(Population Assessment)

Enables monitoring of priority quality and outcome measures across demographic and SDOH groups and 
identifies disparities to guide targeted equity-focused interventions. 
(PHP Health Equity Dashboard & PDS-PMG Health Equity Insights)

Tracks centralized outreach and disease management efforts, including members identified, outreached, 
and engaged, and monitors visit completion and pre/post utilization impact. (Outreach Dashboard)

Measures clinical and utilization outcomes across chronic condition programs, 
comparing engaged vs not engaged and before vs after disease management to assess program effectiveness. 
(Healthy Solutions – Disease Management Outcomes)

Provides detailed visibility into high-need Medicaid populations across 
demographics, cost, utilization, providers, SDOH, and care management to 
support prioritization and intervention planning. (PHP Medicaid CISC Population Dashboard)

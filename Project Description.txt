Member 360:

Executive Summary
This dashboard will serve as an on-demand tool for Member 360, Identification of intervention population and provide tools for initial feasibility assesmnet. Define initial population for interention and provide overview of key metrics.


Data Defination:
All claims – Medical and Rx. Medical claims Include HealthEdge (HRP) – Inpatient, outpatient, professional + All TPAs encounters (Magellan BH claims, Dental, Vision, Capital/Optum Rx, Transportation etc.).
Enriched data from Johns Hopkins ACG grouper (predictive analytics tool) and JIVA care management, limited Epic data for members who are our patients (e.g., Epic Mychart data showing digital engagement), Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) data harvested from Epic, JiVA, HRP, purchased data from Acxoim.


KPI Definitions:
Inpatient Hospitalization Count: Count of Acute care Inpatient stays for causes that are not related to child-birth and injury.

All Cause Inpatient Hospitalizations: Count of acute care inpatient stays asscoiated with admissions for any reason.

Unplanned Inpatient Hospitalizations: Count of acute care inpatient stays not associated with a planned procedure. The unplanned patient hospitalization count field is a subset of the inpatient hospitalization count.

Readmissions 30 Day: Count of the number of acute care inpatient hospitalizations within 30 days of a previous inpatient discharge. The readmission 30 day count field is a subset of the all cause inpatient hospitalization count.

Unplanned Readmissions 30 Day: Count of the number of unplanned acute care inpatient hospitalizations within 30 days of a previous inpatient hospitalization. The unplanned readmission 30 day count field is a subset of the inpatient hospitalization count.

Pharmacy Cost Band: A banded indicator of historic pharmacy costs based upon pharmacy cost percentiles.

Total Cost Band: A banded indicator of historic total costs based upon total cost percentiles.

Rescaled Scores: Unscaled Scores are resclaed so that  mean of the population is 1.0.

Resource Utilization Band: Aggregations of ACGs based upon estimates of concurrent resource that are used to provide a way of separating the population into broad co-morbidity groupings.

Unscaled Concurrent Risk Score: A concurrent total cost risk for this patient for the observation period. Based upon a regression model against a reference population (with a mean of 1.0), the predicted value is expressed as a relative weight.

Age-Gender Concurrent Risk Score: A concurrent weight assigned to this patient based upon their age-sex mix using local cost data. The weight for each age band and gender is calculated as the simple average total cost of all individuals assigned to each category divided by the average total cost of all individuals in the source data file.

Unscaled Total Cost Predicted Risk: The estimated total costs (including pharmacy costs) for this patient for the year following the observation period.

Predicted Pharmacy Cost Range: A dollar range indicating the estimated pharmacy costs for this patient for the year following the observation period.

Probability Persistent High User: Probability that this patient will be in the top 20% of users in the population for four consecutive half year periods.

Probability IP Hospitalization: The probability that this patient will have an inpatient hospitalization in the year following the observation period.

PHP Health Equity Dashboard

Description *
This dashboard was built in support of a 2021 Promise One Presbyterian Leadership Team (OPLT) strategic initiative. 
The initial phase of the project supports segmentation analysis for 14 priority measures selected by Dr. Jason Mitchell, Dr. Norbert Topf, Leigh Caswell and Dr. Darcie Robran-Marquez.

Intended use
The dashboard is intended to help improve equity in health and healthcare outcomes.

Purpose
The purpose of this dashboard is to derive insights into factors that may negatively impact members’ health due to external stresses within the community, 
to the member individually and to improve equity in health and health outcomes

Current Scope
The initial phase of the project supports segmentation analysis for 16 priority HEDIS measures.

Measure Descriptions
Note - Administrative measure end-of-year completion rates are calculated based on data sources accessible to PHP (administrative data only), Hybrid measure end-of-year completion rates are based on a sample population from the denominator with additional data mining (e.g. at a provider's office) for completion status. For Hybrid measures, data in this dashboard is based on administrative data only and may not align with reported end-of-year rates. 

1. Breast Cancer Screening (BCS): Administrative Measure - Assesses women 50–74 years of age who had at least one mammogram to screen for breast cancer in the past two years.
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

2. Childhood Immunization Status (CIS) – Combination 7: Hybrid Measure - Assesses children 2 years of age who had Combination 7 vaccines by their second birthday. Combination 7 includes DTaP, IPV, MMR, HiB, HepB, VZV, PCV, HepA and RV vaccinations. 
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP  

3. Colorectal Cancer Screening (COL): Hybrid Measure - Assesses adults 50–75 who had appropriate screening for colorectal cancer with any of the following tests: annual fecal occult blood test, flexible sigmoidoscopy every 5 years, colonoscopy every 10 years, computed tomography colonography every 5 years, stool DNA test every 3 years. 
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

4. Prenatal And Postpartum Care (PPC): Hybrid Measure - Assesses access to prenatal and postpartum care. Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP 
  a. Postpartum Care: The percentage of deliveries in which women had a postpartum visit on or between 7 and 84 days after delivery.
  b. Timeliness of Prenatal Care: The percentage of deliveries in which women had a prenatal care visit in the first trimester, on or before the enrollment start date or within 42 days of enrollment in the organization.
 
5. Controlling High Blood Pressure(CBP): Hybrid Measure - The percentage of members 18–85 years of age who had a diagnosis of hypertension (HTN) and whose BP was adequately controlled (<140/90 mm Hg) during the measurement year.
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

6. Hemoglobin A1c Control For Patients Diabetes(HBD): Hybrid Measure - The Percentage of members 18–75 years of age with diabetes (types 1 and 2) whose hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) was at the following levels during the measurement year:
·    HbA1c control (<8.0%)
·    HbA1c poor control (>9.0%)
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

7. Child And Adolescent Well Care Visits: Administrative Measure - The percentage of members 3–21 years of age who had at least one comprehensive well-care visit with a PCP or an OB/GYN practitioner during the measurement year.
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

8.  Well Child Visits in the First fifteen (15) Months of Life (W15): Administrative Measure - The percentage of Members who turned fifteen (15) months old during the measurement year and had six (6) or more well-child visits. 
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

9. Weight Assessment and Counseling for Nutrition and Physical Activity for Children/Adolescents (WCC): Hybrid Measure - The percentage of Members ages three (3) through seventeen (17) years of age who had an outpatient visit with a PCP or OB/GYN and who had evidence of counseling for physical activity during the measurement year. 
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

10. Antidepressant Medication Management (AMM): Administrative Measure - The number of Members age eighteen (18) years and older as of April 30 of the measurement year who were diagnosed with a new episode of major depression during the intake period and received at least one-hundred eighty (180) Calendar Days (6 months) of continuous treatment with an antidepressant medication. 
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

11. Initiation and Engagement of Alcohol and Other Drug Dependence Treatment (IET): Administrative Measure - The total percentage of adolescent and adult Members with a new episode of alcohol or other drug (AOD) dependence who received the following; Initiation of AOD Treatment. 
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

12. Follow-Up After Hospitalization for Mental Illness (FUH): Administrative Measure - The percentage of discharges for members 6 years of age and older who were hospitalized for treatment of selected mental illness diagnoses and who had a follow-up visit with a mental health practitioner within 30 days after discharge. 
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP

13. Follow-Up After Emergency Department Visit for Mental Illness (FUM): Administrative Measure - The percentage of emergency department (ED) visits for members 6 years of age and older with a principal diagnosis of mental illness, who had a follow-up visit for mental illness within 30 days of the ED visit.
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP 

14. Diabetes Screening for People With Schizophrenia or Bipolar Disorder Who Are Using Antipsychotic Medications (SSD): Administrative Measure - The percentage of Members 18-64 years of age with schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder or bipolar disorder, who were dispensed an antipsychotic medication and had a diabetes screening test during the measurement year.
Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PHP  

SDOH Definitions
Race – Member self-reported answers to Race question during enrollment or clinical visit 

Ethnicity- Member self-reported answers to Ethnicity question during enrollment or clinical visit

Healthy Lifestyle Rank - The healthy lifestyle score provides a score for member by virtue of their lifestyle habits. It is derived from variables that takes into account food habits, health consciousness and health club memberships
 
Gender/Sex - Member self-reported answers to Gender/Sex question during enrollment or clinical visit 

Food Insecurity – Member answered affirmatively to Food Insecurity questions asked during care assessment or clinical visit or derived from SDOH data elements (Acxiom)

Transportation Insecurity – (1) Member answered affirmatively to Transportation Insecurity questions asked during care assessment or clinical visit, (2) or derived from SDOH data elements (Acxiom), or (3) had claims specific to transportation concerns

Housing Insecurity – Member answered affirmatively to Housing Insecurity questions asked during care assessment or clinical visit or derived from SDOH data elements (Acxiom)

Household Education Score - The household education data is derived from SDOH data elements; educated is college or graduate degree, not educated is high school or lower

Social Support Insecurity – Member answered affirmatively to Social Insecurity questions asked during care assessment or clinical visit or derived from SDOH data elements (Acxiom)

Area Deprivation Index - An Area Deprivation Index (ADI) is a multidimensional evaluation of a region's socioeconomic conditions that have been linked to health outcomes. ADI score ranges from 0-10, 10 being most deprived area 

Social Vulnerability Index - The Social Vulnerability Index (SVI) uses U.S. Census data to determine the social vulnerability of every census tract

Measure Descriptions

1. CDBR:1532 – Pneumonia, CMS – Percent Readmit within 30 Days, Same Facility, Acute Care: Assesses percentage of encounters with an unplanned readmission to the same facility within 30 days among encounters for acute care inpatients with a principal discharge diagnosis of pneumonia or a principal discharge diagnosis of sepsis (not including severe sepsis or septic shock) with a secondary diagnosis of pneumonia or aspiration pneumonia coded as present-on-admission (POA), and no secondary diagnosis of severe sepsis or septic shock coded as POA. Measure source: MIDAS. Population: PDS.

2. CDBR:919 – Inpatients – Percent Readmit to Acute Care within 30 Days: Assesses percentage of encounters for which the patient was readmitted to the same facility within 30 days among all inpatient encounters. Measure source: MIDAS. Population: PDS.

3. CDBR:993 – Sepsis, Severe – Mortality Rate: Assesses mortality rate among acute care inpatient encounters with a principal or secondary discharge diagnosis of severe sepsis. Measure source: MIDAS. Population: PDS.
 
4. CDBR:996 – Septic Shock – Mortality Rate: Assesses mortality rate among acute care inpatient encounters with a principal or secondary discharge diagnosis of septic shock. Measure source: MIDAS. Population: PDS.

5. Opioid Stewardship: Duration: Assesses the percent of acute pain patients with outpatient and discharge opioid prescription orders having a duration > 7 days. Acute pain patients are defined as having < 60 days duration of opioids ordered in the previous 6 months. Cough syrups with codeine and similar medications were excluded from this calculation. Measure source: Epic Clarity. Population: PDS.
 
6. Opioid Stewardship: Morphine Milligram Equivalent (MME): Assesses the total daily dosage of outpatient and discharge opioid prescription orders to patients identified using the last start date of a medication in a month. The dose at the start of therapy was chosen for the MME calculation and does not account for upward or downward tapering of doses. For overlapping therapy (orders with the same start date), MME is a total of all unique medications; however, not all variations are accounted for. Orders for Buprenorphine and Cough/Cold Preparations are not included. Measure source: Epic Clarity. Population: PDS.
 
7. Opioid Stewardship: Naloxone: Assesses the percent of patients with outpatient and discharge opioid prescription orders having an opioid antagonist (Naloxone) order in the previous 12 months. Only opioid orders with a duration of >= 5 days are included and orders buprenorphine-naloxone are not included in the numerator but are included in the denominator. Measure source: Epic Clarity. Population: PDS.

8. Breast Cancer Screening (BCS): Assesses women 50–74 years of age who had at least one mammogram to screen for breast cancer in the past two years. Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PMG Capitated. 

9. Comprehensive Diabetes Care (CDC) – HbA1c Control: Assesses adults age 18-75 with a diagnosis of diabetes (Type 1 and Type 2) who had HbA1c control (< 8.0%) during the measurement year as identified by claim/encounter or automated laboratory data. Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PMG Capitated. 

10. Childhood Immunization Status (CIS) – Combination 7: Assesses children 2 years of age who had Combination 7 vaccines by their second birthday. Combination 7 includes DTaP, IPV, MMR, HiB, HepB, VZV, PCV, HepA and RV vaccinations. Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PMG Capitated. 

11. Colorectal Cancer Screening (COL): Assesses adults 50–75 who had appropriate screening for colorectal cancer with any of the following tests: annual fecal occult blood test, flexible sigmoidoscopy every 5 years, colonoscopy every 10 years, computed tomography colonography every 5 years, stool DNA test every 3 years. Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PMG Capitated. 

12. Prenatal and Postpartum Care (PPC): Assesses access to prenatal and postpartum care. Measure source: Inovalon Data Mart. Population: PMG Capitated.

Official Statistic page of the Dashboard has:
Do Statistically Significant Differences in Health Outcomes Rates Exist Among Demographic and SDOH Groups?
Introduction 
This page shows demographic and SDOH groups that have statistically significant differences in health outcomes rates. Please refer to the Welcome page for a full list of measure definitions.

Methodology 
The largest factor category, in terms of patient count, is designated the reference category for all demographic and SDOH factors except ADI, for which "Quintile 1" is always the reference category. Measures with a binary outcome (e.g. Readmissions) are evaluated using a logistic regression model. Measures with a continuous outcome (e.g. MME) are evaluated using a linear regression model. Resultant p-values for demographic or SDOH categories that are < 0.05 are considered statistically significant. All others, excluding reference categories, are considered nonsignificant. 
> Significant p-value < 0.05 
> Nonsignificant p-value: >= 0.05 

Inclusions & Exclusions 
Health outcomes data are available over a rolling three-year period in the Overview tab. In this page, significant differences among demographic and SDOH groups was determined over a shorter period. Only patients that meet each measure's denominator inclusion criteria were evaluated. 
> HEDIS measures: evaluated at year-end 2023 
> MIDAS and Opioid Stewardship measures: evaluated for year 2023

Interpretation 
Significant: The outcome event was significantly more or less likely to occur for this group compared to the reference group (blue bars) when controlling for other demographic and SDOH factors. ‘Significantly more likely’ and ‘significantly less likely’ can be good/better (green bar) or bad/worse (orange bar) depending on the health outcome measure.  
Nonsignificant: The outcome event was not more or less likely (grey bars) to occur for this group compared to the reference group (blue bars). 
> Significantly Better categories: green 
> Significantly Worse categories: orange 
> Nonsignificant categories:  grey 
> Reference categories: blue 

Example 
Among BMI Group categories for the All-Cause Readmissions measure, the "Underweight" group is significantly more likely, and the "Obese" and "Unknown" groups are significantly less likely to experience readmission than the "Normal/Overweight" reference population. More likely to experience readmission is undesirable, so "Underweight" is coded orange; less likely to experience readmission is desirable, so "Obese" and "Unknown" are coded green.

Summary

Care Management 
Member care management level by region and selected category of eligibility (COE). 

Important Definitions
RUB
Resource Utilization Bands (RUBs) is a collapse of ACGs by the combination of concurrent relative resources used and the number of comorbidities.  ACGs are health status categories, created by Johns Hopkins ACG System, defined by morbidity, age, and sex. 

GII 
Global Impactibility Index is a statistic created by Presbyterian Healthcare Services which: (1) measures the of overall “impact ability” of a member based on their health care utilization (retrospective and prospective); (2) current level of care coordination and; (3) historic pharmacy adherence as reported through ACG variables. This statistic allows prioritization of initiatives to members. Higher GII number indicates higher impactibility through intervention.
